## About
Hi! My name is Rizky Kusuma and I'm currently a sophomore at a senior high in Indonesia.
My primary interest has been on Physics Olympiads, but recently, I've been building a problem solving model to aid that.
This year, I qualified for the Indonesian Physics Olympiad (INAPhO) Semi Final round (#67/1196). I hope to someday participate at the International Physics Olympiad (IPhO).
## Projects
Earlier this year I noticed how inefficient my studying method was. I was spending hours working through chapters, only to barely solve a third of the problems correctly. Obviously I was frustrated. But I wasn't ready to give up yet, and this led me to read some articles in Physics Education Research (PER). Namely, [Heller and Reif (1984)](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1207/s1532690xci0102_2) and [Chi, Feltovich, Glaser (1981)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1207/s15516709cog0502_2). These articles inspired me to develop my own problem solving "framework". At first, however, it was still vague to me what constitutes a "good olympiad physics problem solving framework"; so I began analyzing solutions from [the toughest high school physics olympiad](https://physicscup.ee/), and everything clicked from there.
